Switchblade® 600

Loitering Munition

Destroy Hardened Targets

The next generation of extended-range loitering munitions, Switchblade 600 delivers unprecedented RSTA support and lethal punch. It is equipped with an anti-armor warhead for engaging larger, hardened targets at standoff distances of 40 km and more. And its class-leading, high-resolution EO/IR sensors and precision flight control provide accurate targeting over its 40-minute endurance.

Whether launched from fixed defensive positions, combat vehicles, or aerial systems, Switchblade 600 provides field commanders tactical options for a variety of mission profiles.

Key Features

All-In-One Tube-Launched System

Switchblade 600 ships in a self-contained tube that includes everything needed to launch, fly, and prosecute targets without additional launcher gear. Setup is rapid typically under 10 minutes allowing operators to deploy quickly from ground positions, vehicles, or integrated platforms.

Extended Loitering & Standoff Range

With more than 40 minutes of loiter time and a baseline range beyond 40 km, it provides persistent presence over target areas and reduces the need for external ISR assets. Optional data-link extensions can push engagements toward 90 km via relays.

High-Resolution EO/IR Targeting

Equipped with a stabilized, high-resolution electro-optical/infrared sensor suite, the system can detect and track both static and moving targets, feeding real-time video back to the operator for precise target identification and geolocation.

Precision Strike Against Armored Targets

The vehicle carries an anti-armor warhead capable of engaging hardened static and moving vehicles, bringing localized precision firepower to tactical units traditionally reliant on larger indirect-fire systems.

Encrypted Control & Resilient Navigation

Integrated data links and secure GPS help assure command and control in contested signal environments, maintaining responsiveness and mission integrity under electronic warfare conditions.
